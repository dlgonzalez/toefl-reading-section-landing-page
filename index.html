What's New in 2026?

The TOEFL iBT has undergone major changes. Here's everything you need to know about the new format.

1

Multistage Adaptive Testing

Reading and Listening sections now use adaptive testing. Your performance in Module 1 determines the difficulty of Module 2.

Module 1 (Routing)
Mixed difficulty · Determines your Module 2 level
Module 2 (Final)
Easier or harder based on Module 1 performance
2

New 1–6 Band Scoring Scale

The old 0–120 scale has been replaced with a 1–6 band scale aligned with CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference).

1.0–1.5
A1 · Below Basic
2.0–2.5
A2 · Basic
3.0–3.5
B1 · Intermediate
4.0–4.5
B2 · Upper-Int.
5.0–5.5
C1 · Advanced
6.0
C2 · Proficient
3

New Task Types

The 2026 TOEFL introduces new task types that reflect real-world academic and campus-life English.

📖 Reading: Complete the Words
Fill in missing letters in academic paragraphs (10 blanks)
✍️ Writing: Build a Sentence
Drag-and-drop word chunks to form grammatically correct sentences
🎤 Speaking: Listen and Repeat
Repeat 7 spoken sentences of increasing complexity
📖 Reading: Select the Sentence (NEW)
Click the sentence that matches a functional description
4

Shorter, More Focused Test

The total test time has been reduced to under 2 hours, making it more efficient while maintaining rigorous assessment.

Old Format (pre-2026)
3–4 hours
New Format (2026)
~2 hours
5

Real-World Academic & Campus-Life Focus

All tasks now reflect authentic language use in university settings and everyday campus life.

✓ Emails, notices, social media posts
✓ Conversations, announcements
✓ Academic passages (no specialized knowledge required)
✓ Multiple accents (North America, UK, Australia)

Understanding Your Scores

The new scoring system provides clearer, more intuitive feedback on your English proficiency level.

📊 Section Scores

Each section (Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking) receives a score on the 1–6 scale, reported in half-point increments (e.g., 4.0, 4.5, 5.0).

Example Score Report:
Reading: 5.0 (C1)
Listening: 4.5 (B2)
Writing: 4.0 (B2)
Speaking: 5.5 (C1)

🎯 Overall Score

Your overall score is the average of your 4 section scores, rounded to the nearest half band.

Calculation Example:
(5.0 + 4.5 + 4.0 + 5.5) ÷ 4 = 4.75
Rounded to nearest half: 5.0
Overall Band: 5.0 (C1)

🔄 Dual Score Reporting (Transition Period)

For a 2-year transition period, score reports will include both the new 1–6 scale and a comparable score on the old 0–120 scale. This helps institutions adapt to the new format.

New Scale
1–6
Legacy Scale (for reference)
0–120
